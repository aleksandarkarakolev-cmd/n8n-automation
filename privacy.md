# Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This app ("n8n automation") is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

## What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app accesses the owner's own Google Sheets, Google Drive files that the owner selects, and the owner's own YouTube channel: channel statistics, analytics, comments on the owner's videos, and video uploads.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to automate the owner's own channel: to upload the owner's videos, to reply to comments on the owner's videos, and to save statistics and reports to the owner's own Google Sheet. Comment text may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic Claude) to draft a reply.

## Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer (n8n) and in the owner's own Google Sheet. It is not sold or shared with anyone else. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
